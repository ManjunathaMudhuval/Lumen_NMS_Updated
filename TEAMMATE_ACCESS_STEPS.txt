Test Info NMS - Steps for Teammate to Open the App
====================================================
You do NOT need to install anything, clone any code, or use a
terminal. Just follow these steps:

STEP 1: Connect to the company VPN
-------------------------------------
Make sure you are connected to the company VPN: roc.centurylink.com
This is the same VPN used to reach the jump server "zunidev"
(zuni-dev-01), the machine actually running the app, at internal IP
10.237.67.2.

(Some VPN setups only allow SSH-style jump access and do not route
general HTTP traffic to the jump server's IP - if step 2 fails, this
is the most likely reason. See the troubleshooting note below.)

STEP 2: Open the link in your browser
----------------------------------------
Open any web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge) and go to:

    http://10.237.67.2:8091

STEP 3: Use the app
----------------------
You should see the "Test Info NMS" dashboard load automatically with
live data (Dashboard, Inventory, Device Details, Device Config, etc.)
Just click around the left-side menu to explore.

If the page does not load:
-----------------------------
1. Double check you are on the company VPN (Step 1).
2. Try a hard refresh: Ctrl + Shift + R
3. If your VPN only lets you SSH/RDP into zunidev as a jump server
   (common setup) rather than routing general web traffic to it, a
   direct http://10.237.67.2:8091 link will NOT work for you even
   though you are "connected". Use the SSH tunnel method below instead.
4. Contact the sender - a firewall port may need to be opened on
   their end, or the app may not currently be running.


FALLBACK METHOD: SSH Tunnel (use if the direct link in Step 2 fails)
=======================================================================
If you have SSH access to the zunidev jump server (ask the sender for
your username on that machine if you do not already have one), you
can reach the app through an SSH tunnel instead of a direct IP link.
This works even if your VPN only allows SSH-style jump access.

1. Open a terminal on your own computer (not on zunidev) and run:

    ssh -L 8091:127.0.0.1:8091 <your-username>@10.237.67.2

   Replace <your-username> with your actual login name on zunidev.
   Keep this terminal window open - closing it disconnects the tunnel.

2. Open your browser and go to:

    http://127.0.0.1:8091

   This now shows the same live app, tunneled securely through the
   jump server, without needing the app's network traffic to be
   routed directly to you.

Notes:
--------
- This link only works while the sender's app is running on their
  machine. If the sender restarts their computer or stops the app,
  the link will stop working until they start it again.
- You are viewing the live running app directly - there is nothing to
  download, install, or set up on your side.
